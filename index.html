Because the class weights are balanced, the score is a model output tuned to catch the positive class, not a calibrated probability. The Low (0-35), Medium (35-65) and High (65-100) bands apply to this score.

Not medical advice. This page was built for machine learning education. The dataset comes from women of Pima Indian heritage aged 21 and over, so results may not apply to other groups. Talk to a doctor about any health decision.
